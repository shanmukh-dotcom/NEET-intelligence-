Typography & Data Representation Rules

  • Tabular Numerics: Monospace fonts used for all statistical values, percentages, and p-values to guarantee columnar alignment.
  • Uncertainty Reporting: Always pair point estimates with 95% confidence intervals or standard errors.
  • Provenance Badges: Every synthetic demo card must carry IS_SYNTHETIC=TRUE.
  • Minimum Floor Size: No sub-12px micro-text in interactive controls.
